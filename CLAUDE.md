## Hukum dasar motion (always on, no need to be asked)
1. **Knowledge first.** Before any motion work read the motion memories, the `motion-taste` skill and the `motion-docs` NOTES the piece needs. The user explains a concept; you direct it with these formulas.
2. **Continuous 3D Flow over Static Posters (Unshackled Enterprise Craft).** Write the formula rasa (idea, spatial metaphor, 3D camera journey, FG/MG/BG depth layers, lighting & texture treatment, type choreography) and test a 3-second continuous motion pass. Styleframes are frames caught mid-flow, judged with `eye.py poster --flow` (≥ 70): it relaxes focal stars, collage density, edge bleed and photo colours, while neon/glow, mud and value contrast stay as strict as the kiblat (neon ≈ 1 %, 1 accent). Never reduce a video into static "poster-per-poster" slides or flat 3-color boxes just to chase a still-frame heuristic: rich multi-layer collages, real brand icons/cutouts, volumetric lighting/bloom, foreground parallax bokeh, and oversized bleeding typography / camera fly-throughs are encouraged.
3. **Motif lock & Enterprise Polish.** Combine cohesive signature techniques (e.g., 3D camera portal fly-through + multi-plane collage parallax + tactile kinetic typography) with discipline and intentionality—wild and experimental in camera & spatial transitions, enterprise-clean in typography legibility, physics curves, and audio sync.
4. **See what you made (Craft & Physics Auditor, not Print Policeman).** After every render run `python -I .claude/skills/motion-eye/scripts/eye.py sense <render> [--flow]` (add EffectCraft `frame_review` only when EffectCraft was used) to audit real motion craft (easing curves, impulse/hold rhythm, audio-visual sync, safe-zone legibility on key holds, and technical defects). Open the sheets — numbers are only half of seeing.
5. **Originality.** References teach craft only; concept, script, world, palette and signature transition are ours.
5b. **Kiblat.** `my-ref/` holds the videos whose DNA, feel, SFX, motion, typography, elements and angles Ikhsan loves. Their combined taste is `my-ref/_eye/kiblat/` (`kiblat.json`, `kiblat.md`) and `motion-taste/references/kiblat-ikhsan.md`. When a brief has no specific reference, the kiblat is the reference: targets from `kiblat.json`, review with `--ref my-ref/_eye/kiblat/kiblat.json`. Learn its DNA; never copy a video from it.

7. **Secrets** stay in `.env.local` (never printed, never committed).
