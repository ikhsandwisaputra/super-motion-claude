# Lessons learned from real reviews

Each lesson is a correction the client actually gave, or a bug found in self-review, written as symptom → cause → rule. Apply the rules from the first draft; don't wait to be told again.

## Direction and taste
| client note | cause | rule now |
|---|---|---|
| "aneh, jelek, jedug-jedug, music kacau kayak editan anak SD" | generated 120 BPM anthem with a boom on every beat; no measured craft | Bed = low pad/drone that ducks under the VO and cuts to silence before punchlines. No beat booms. Learn a reference's craft by measuring it. |
| "harapan gue kedepannya gak lagi plagiat konten referensi, itu murni untuk belajar" | the study film rebuilt the reference phrase by phrase | References are for learning only. Every film gets its own concept, script, world, palette and signature transition (`originality.md`); run the similarity check. |
| "tipografi masih kaku dan plain, lurus tok asal muncul… After Effects terkenal dengan tipografi angle-nya" | words were flat 2D, appearing without camera or depth | Words live in 3D: tilted blocks, depth, a type camera with dolly/orbit/roll, entrance variety (fly/flip/rise/drop/swing/whip/slam/arc/focus). |
| "ada tulisan di meja… kita gagal ngikut kamera" | no world-space text | Some phrases lie on surfaces (desk, wall) as world-space planes that follow the set camera. |
| "blur motion masih kurang" | blur only on a few entrances | Real motion blur: 3–6 sub-frames per frame, 180° shutter, linear-light average. 6 samples during moves, slams and transitions. |
| "pencahayaan, saturasi, efek keseluruhannya masih kurang" (v1) and later "kontrasnya terlalu terang, tajam di mata" (v3) | first too flat, then over-corrected | Aim for a soft premium grade: contrast ~0.14, saturation ~1.07, soft gaussian bloom, grain ~0.02, warm highlights. Practical lights glow but never blow out behind text. |
| "kalimat selesai → ada perpindahan angle kamera… ini teknik yang harus lu kuasai" | each phrase replaced the last in place | Camera travel: every phrase has its own place in 3D; when one finishes, the camera pans/yaws/rolls/dollies to the next (inOutCubic 0.4–0.55 s, full motion blur); the finished phrase recedes (backAt: back in z, blurred, dimmer). |
| "marker merah harusnya tiap kata, bukan cuma 'Claude'" | highlight box on one word | A highlight marker follows the VO word by word and leaves when the next word lands. |
| "masih menimpa dan terlalu menempel (MOTION / video)" | layout by eye only | Run the projected overlap check; keep breathing room between words. |
| "tulisan jangan terlalu miring" | tilts of 15–25° | Global tilt damping: rx/ry ×0.5, rz ×0.6. Keep authored tilts modest. |
| "kurang popup mulusnya" | underdamped springs (ζ 0.35–0.5) and 4-frame slides | Pop-ins use ζ 0.6–0.7, slides use quint-out over ~7 frames, alpha ramps over ~3 frames, plus a 0.9→1 scale pop. |
| "sfx tulisan typonya ditambahkan, ganti-ganti enak didengar" | only transitions had SFX | Every word gets a typo SFX matched to its entrance, with the variant and ±6% pitch picked per word, 18–26 dB under the VO. |
| "neon sampah, kinetic teks tidak enak, font jelek, mata melihatnya apaan ini, asal jadi, konsepnya tidak menyatu… rumusnya itu loh kurang" (video-3) | measured: one dark navy field for 35 s (1 colour field vs 8 in the reference), contrast Δ24 vs 67, 88 % muddy frames, 5 accent hues with glow on one field, 3 type families mixed in phrases, hero 4.5–7 % of frame height, a visual change every 5.4 s vs 1.85 s; the metaphor never became a visible world; every arsenal technique was added; QA checked defects, not taste | Formula rasa + colour script + 3–4 styleframes with `eye.py poster` ≥ 70 before animating; motif lock (≤ 3 signature techniques); 2 families, hero ≥ 3× support; `eye.py sense` after every render and `eye.py review --ref` ≥ 80 before any preview (`motion-taste`, `motion-eye`, case file `motion-taste/references/case-video3.md`). |
| "transisinya gak ada" (video-3) | section turns (glyph recompile, caret and diff wipes) happened inside the same dark field; camera travel between phrases crossed a uniform background with no parallax cues | A section turn must change the frame: a new colour field (ΔE ≥ 25), a new set, or a big camera move through a lit, layered world. |
| "sfx digedein lagi … di video 4 kurang kedenger terutama sfx typography nya itu komponen penting" | typo SFX at 18–26 dB under the VO and ducked a further −3 dB under words; inaudible on laptop/phone speakers | Word/typography SFX sit ≈ 5–9 dB under the VO's voiced level (60 ms RMS), keys ≈ 8–10 dB; duck the music under the VO, not the SFX (−1 dB at most); measure every SFX class in the level table and trim per class (`aset/video-5/tools/mix5.py` TRIM). |
| "tempo video nya terlalu agak lambat … ciri khas motion itu perpindahannya agak cepat gak slow phase" (video-5 hook, SKOR RASA 92 but burst 11 %) | kiblat-calm physics (0.7 s window opens, 1.9 s dolly zooms, 0.36 s word entrances), 5 s of typing, 0.4–0.7 s VO gaps; "burst low = sengaja" was accepted by the eye but not by the client | Burst ≥ 20 % is a real target: transitions 0.3–0.45 s, word entrances ≈ 0.26 s with 2-frame stagger, camera moves ≈ 1 s, VO gaps 0.1–0.35 s (a ≈ 5 % atempo is fine), never a near-empty frame while a slow VO word plays (give it anticipation — a hanging word, a rolling slot). A longer film gets more beats, not slower ones ("genapin semenit": 43 s → 60 s with six skill beats and a VO-less flex). |
| "subtitlenya taruh dibawah fotonya deh biar enak dibaca … masih berantakan teksnya" (Symphony Nation eps 06 v1) | caption on top, the name slam on top, the fact under the card: three text places, the eye jumped between them | One fixed reading order per film: big type in one top zone (name → fact), the hero image, the caption in ONE panel right under the image (the old series' approved "caption menempel di foto"), the speaker badge bottom-left. |
| "mepet juga sama garis" (same film) | caption on top + camera pushes up to 14 % brought text within ~12 px of the header's progress line | Keep ≥ 40 px between any text and a fixed UI line at the largest camera push; when type lives near the HUD, push ≤ 6 %. |
| "skill film motion supermu mana gue ga liat di video ini?" (same film; video-3 asked the same) | all the craft was invisible: SDF morph, value floods, springs, sub-frame blur, karaoke — measured fine (SKOR 80) but nothing read as a "motion move" | Every film needs 1–2 VISIBLE signature moves on its peaks, inside the motif lock: kinetic letters on the keyword (fly-in, smear, tracking collapse, landing shake), a camera dive with zoom blur and speed-reactive optics, a 3D word flip. Invisible craft alone reads as "no skill". |

## Bugs found in self-review (symptom → cause → fix)
| symptom | cause | fix |
|---|---|---|
| A "new" encode measured exactly like the previous one; the frames on disk were the old version (Symphony eps 06) | the render failed at start-up (one image load) but `render.py … \| tail -3` swallowed the error and its exit code, so encode ran on stale frames | Never pipe a render through `tail`/`grep` without checking: keep its log and exit code, and before encoding confirm every frame's mtime is newer than the render start. Requests for files that don't exist (a missing flag) must not be made at all, so a non-zero exit always means a real failure. |
| A letter or a marker box flickers between states | word texture cached as "settled" before per-letter effects finished, then repainted only when blur changed | Repaint until `max(16, letters×stagger+10, dur+6, tintDelay+10, boxOff+8)` frames. |
| Faint ghost copies / "burik" around all text | post glow sampled in a ring of 16 taps | Use a real bloom: bright-pass downsample + separable gaussian. |
| Red text flashes white for two frames | per-item motion blur drew 7 samples at full alpha with additive blend | Pass alpha 1/K into the sample draw, or (now) use frame-level supersampling. |
| Two shots blended on the first frame after a hard cut | sub-frames reached back into the previous shot | Clamp sub-frame times to the current shot unless inside a transition window. |
| A character explodes into pixels at every shot start | pose logic treated the first key as having a previous pose | `prev` is only the key before the current one. |
| Black wedges at the frame edge during camera roll | 2D background drawn without overscan | Overscan the background (×1.16) and couple it to the camera at a fraction (parallax). |
| Shockwave ring drifts away from the feet when the camera pans | ring drawn in background space | Project effect anchors through the type camera (`extra(g, t, P)`). |
| White text vanishes on a bright softbox | text block placed over a practical light | Put text on dark parts of the set, dim the practical, or add a local gradient. |
| Text lying on a desk sinks into the surface | idle "life" tilt applied to surface text | Surface text is `still` and sits a hair above the surface. |
| Text cropped at frame edges after a camera move | perspective + travel enlarge/shift words | Check safe areas at the settled moment AND just before the next camera move. |
| A checker reported "not a function" at random | evaluated `window.ready` before the module had set it | Wait for `window.ready !== undefined` first. |
| A monitor that plays the film itself broke the live frame | snapshot render reused the live stage | Render snapshots on a second stage; run the monitor UI before the shot sets its own room view. |
| Words appeared up to 0.26 s late ("So…") and a breath landed mid-word (video-3, `eleven_v4`) | the aligner lumps trailing silence into a word's end and starts the next word late; a splice placed by alignment times fell inside speech | `vo_edit.py` snaps every silence run to close the word before it and open the word after it; `insert_before_word` splices at the quietest 10 ms around the boundary (print its dBFS, keep it under about −45); `nudge_start` for onsets with no silence (a list read without pauses). |
| A fast thin caret wipe showed as a comb of 5 bars (video-3) | sub-frame motion blur draws K separate copies of an object that moves farther per sub-frame than its own width | Anything thin that crosses the frame in a few frames must be at least `speed × shutter / K` wide (a block caret), or draw its own trail. |
| Slam flash washed the whole frame mint (video-3 self-review) | flash 0.42 on a dark, soft grade | On soft premium grades keep slam flashes ≤ 0.26 → 0.14 → 0.05; the hit comes from the oversized word, shake and CA, not a white-out. |
| Every compile word used the same SFX 18 times | one sample per entrance type | Give each entrance a pool of 2–3 sounds and never play the same sample twice in a row (`kit.js` lastTypo). |
| Gaps before SURPRISED / DIRECTORS measured −32…−35 dBFS instead of silence (video-3) | the bed was reverbed after the sections were summed, so tails rang into the gaps | `mix.py` gates the reverbed bed with the section mask (15 ms fades): every gap between bed sections is digital silence. Measure bed+SFX alone in the gap; VO breath is fine. |
| A 7-colour clutter frame and the video-3 look as a still both "passed" the poster test (73 / 69) — found by the first taste replay (2026-10-29) | the poster score summed points, so strong focus + type paid for failed contrast, colour, space and zones | A frame failing 3 of the 5 fundamentals (kontras, warna, fokus, ruang, tipe) is capped at 64. After any change to the eye's rules run `motion-eye/scripts/replay.py`; a red replay blocks the change. |
| A 60 s ElevenLabs score (music_v2, 8-chunk composition plan) turned into a clipped noise wall for 16 s that swallowed the minimal and kinetic chunks (video-4) | the "brutal: aggressive breakbeat, distorted midrange stabs" chunk drifted into a distorted loop: spectral flatness 0.54, 0.4–0.65 % clipped samples, constant −8.5 dB RMS | Profile every composed track per chunk (RMS per 0.5 s, flatness, clipped %) before mixing; never ask for "distorted"; put "noise wall, white noise, vocals, singing" in each chunk's negative styles (`force_instrumental` only works with a prompt, not with a plan); regenerate only the broken span as its own short plan and splice it inside a transition (60 ms fades under the whoosh). |
| Words appeared 0.2–0.4 s early ("Linear", "Everything", "Minimal", "Depth") and vo_edit made "Everything" 0.14 s long (video-4) | the TTS aligner started those words on a breath or on leading silence; vo_edit then closed the word on the silence after the breath | `aset/video-4/tools/place_vo.py`: onsets from the take's own energy (≥ 30 ms above p99 − 22 dB, walk back over the consonant), ends from the last non-silent hop; cut the take per phrase, place each clip in film time, and derive chapter turns from the words (`build/chapters.json`) so a re-cut VO re-times the whole film. |
| Hook stills passed the poster test, yet 5/8 typed frames failed double-triple and one paused frame scored 64 (video-4) | kiblat-style tiny set-ups (3.5 % of frame height on a 90 %-empty field) used for the very first seconds, and a cursor that stayed 2 s after its job became a second star | The first 10 s carry a hero ≥ 8 % of frame height (1080p: ≈ 150 px Archivo, ≈ 190 px Fraunces italic — the eye reads ≈ 0.55 × font px); small set-ups belong to later beats; a prop leaves within 0.4 s of finishing its action. The hook went 64 → 94 and the tell cleared. |
| A lilac pastel field rendered almost white (50 % pure-white pixels) (video-4) | chroma-keyed bloom with low thresholds (0.1–0.4) still keyed the light pastel field | Pastel fields key bloom on high chroma only (`bloomCLo` 0.5, `bloomCHi` 0.75); check the field in a still before trusting the hex. |
| A ring of wrapped type in amber read as neon (poster: 55 % of the bright ink neon) (video-4) | the accent colour was used as glowing type | The accent is the LIGHT (key light, glow behind the subject); wrapped type stays neutral ivory/grey; bloom keyed to saturated light. |
| Seen from inside a cylinder, every word of the wrapped type read mirrored (video-4) | inner faces show the outside print from behind | Give the BackSide mesh a mirrored copy of the texture (`repeat.x = -1`) with its offset kept in sync. |
| A thin 3D spring curve (tube radius 4.5) combed into stacked copies during camera travel (video-4) | 6 sub-frames of a stroke that moves farther than its width per sub-frame | Show thin strokes only while the camera is slow (reveal the curve in the pull-back) or draw their own trail. |
| A 59 s ElevenLabs score (music_v2, 5-chunk plan timed to the VO) put its drop at 16.0 s instead of the planned 10.1 s and merged the last two chunks (video-6) | music_v2 treats chunk `duration_ms` as a hint, not a grid | Profile the score (RMS per 0.5 s, flatness, clipped %, kick onsets) and find its beat grid (autocorrelation of spectral flux); then re-cut it in the mix on bar boundaries: map each film section to a source range so the downbeat lands on the key word (`aset/video-6/tools/mix6.py` SEGS), with digital-silence gates and a low-pass sweep for a breakdown. No re-generation needed. |
| "Logbook." and "Spreadsheet." were placed 0.9 s early, inside the narrator's dramatic pauses (video-6, eleven_v3) | after a long pause the aligner starts the next word at the end of the previous one; `onset()` only searched +0.6 s past the aligned start | `place_vo.py` searches for the real onset up to the next word's aligned start (+0.3 s, max 1.4 s). Check single-word clips: a "word" longer than ~0.8 s is a misplaced onset, not a slow word. |
| Measured burst stayed 11–12 % although every shot had pop-ins and snap pushes (video-6) | `eye.py` burst = mean whole-frame difference ≥ 3.5 % between frames 0.1 s apart; small UI moves on a light field barely register; white→pale or white→white turns merge into one long "shot" | Tempo you can measure comes from whole-frame change: trucks between spots on one board (0.34 s snap + post blur matched to the speed), value flips light ↔ dark at every section turn, big elements entering with real travel (cards from below, pills from three sides), slot rolls instead of in-place swaps. Video-6 went 11 → 16–17 % burst and shot median 2.8 → 2.5 s that way, SKOR RASA 92. |
| Text inside a fading modal/card stayed fully opaque (video-6 self-review) | `text()`/`phrase()`/UI components set `globalAlpha` absolutely and reset it to 1 | Components multiply the parent alpha (`prev * a`) and restore `prev`, never 1. |
| Poster test counted 4 colours on white UI frames: "biru-ungu" + "cyan-biru" (video-6) | the app's UI blue #1A56DB (hue 221°) and brand Primary #0170E4 (hue 210°) sit in different hue bins | One blue family per light frame: in-app UI keeps the app's colour; the film's own marks on those frames use the brand colour of the same hue (Dark #0740B4); Primary only as a full field or the CTA. |
| A fast-growing circular portal showed as stepped rings (video-6) | its edge travels ~100 px per sub-frame; 6 sub-frames of a hard edge comb | Draw fast-growing shapes with a speed-blurred edge (`softDisc`: band = edge travel per frame × 0.3), or give the window ≥ 10 sub-frames; trucks also get a post-pass directional blur equal to the travel in half a frame. |
| Client: "itu kamu pakai kemampuan supermotion ga?" after the hook (video-3) | the hook used springs, variable-font weight and choreography but skipped velocity distortion, domain warp and rack focus | Walk the arsenal list as a MENU before the first preview: choose the max 3 signature techniques that serve the formula rasa (motif lock), report technique → NOTES file, and say which ones were left out and why. Never "upgrade semua": stacking every technique in video-3 produced exactly the template look the user rejected. |
| A brief's grid (141 BPM, "102 f per bar") and lyric lines ("bust down the skeleton", "12-seater") did not match the song (video-8) | the brief was written from memory; the real song measures 139.0 BPM (multi-bar autocorrelation) and the official lyrics differ | Measure the song's BPM before any frame maths (`aset/video-8/tools/beatgrid.py`) and take on-screen words from the user's lyric sheet + a local transcription (`tools/transcribe.py`, faster-whisper, no credits); say what changed and why. |
| A lyric video's splices: whisper word starts ran ~0.15 s early ("SUV" 62.70 vs the vocal onset 62.86) (video-8) | aligners start words on breaths/lead-ins | Place splices in the gaps measured in the vocal band (500-3500 Hz, 20 ms hops) and keep every segment's song-film offset a whole number of beats (`aset/video-8/tools/music_edit.py`). |
| A 7x slit-scan zoom entrance of a light word on a red field gave 4 flashes in one second (video-8) | the O alone filled the frame light, then the bands swapped it back to red within 0.1 s | Big-scale entrances of light type over a saturated field start at <= ~3x and fade in over ~0.12 s; run `eye.py sense` and read "kedip maks" after every such move. |
| Flat brand colours rendered dull in three.js (orange #FF4F00 → brick, yellow → khaki) (video-11) | the shared renderer used AgX tone mapping (set for video-9's PBR metal); canvas-texture planes went through the tone curve | Flat print / UI films: `renderer.toneMapping = NoToneMapping` (or `material.toneMapped = false`); check a field's hex in a still before trusting it. |
| The "CODE RED" slam was never seen (video-11) | the comic camera left for the next panel 0.4 s before the next line, exactly when the last word of the panel slammed | Leave a panel late and travel fast: arrive 0.1 s after the next line starts, travel 0.3 s; the last word of a panel stays on screen. |
| A loop's quiet zone (a dot + a 26 px caption for ~3 s) scored 76: dark mud, camera dead (video-11) | the loop seam needs an empty "frame 0", so the frames around it were left empty | Keep the seam frame simple but alive: pings on every sung syllable, a slow camera push that crosses the seam, a big mono caption (≈ 84 px) — all functions of seam time so both sides match. Score 76 → 80, seam diff still equal to a neighbour step. |
