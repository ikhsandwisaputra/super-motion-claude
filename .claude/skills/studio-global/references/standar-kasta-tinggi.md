## 2. L1: channel signatures (fixed core + one variable axis each)
Every signature has a **core** (what makes it ours: never changes) and **axes** (what a film may change: pick one per
film, write it on the G0 card).

### S1 Satu Baris (his: intro zoom berbaris + blur)
- Core: one row in depth, one item in focus at a time, blur grows with distance from the focus plane, items alternate
  sides, the camera pulls back between locks and never stops in a hold.
- Axes: row **material** (photos, real objects, documents, UI screens) · row **path** (straight, racing-line curve that
  banks into the turn, helix around the camera, vertical fall through layers, along a map route) · the **door** at the
  end (RNC: lime flood; a film may use its own metaphor as the door) · the **focus device** (lens §3a).
- Where: the EP hook, every chapter's opener, list beats ("tiga hal").

### S2 Bukti Stabilo (his: artikel SS perspektif + stabilo)
- Core: the page takes the frame on a tilted plane, the world drops ~90 % dark behind it, a lime stroke lands on the
  narrator's word, readable hold ≥ 2.5 s, source tab on the page.
- Axes: how the stroke is made (§3b) · how the camera reads (static tilt, rail along the line) · what happens after
  (whip off, the phrase lifts off as type, luma wipe into the next station, tone-layer lift onto the data card) ·
  how many sources (one page, a fanned stack, a sphere of sources that flattens one into focus).
- 1–2 per chapter, never every article.

### S3 Palette and grade
Ink / ivory / one lime accent (#C8F53C; accent text on light #4B6800), neon ≈ 1 %. People and places get the
documentary treatment (neutral grade, 12 fps gate weave 1–2 px, clipped grain, halation). Hits from `tx/` keep only
the accent hue band.

### S4 Station row + spine object
Every idea is a station on one row; next stations' tags glow ahead (α .15); each film has one spine object that states
its structure (balance, split-flap, Peta Jalur). Axis: the spine object is always new and comes from the theme.

### S5 Chapter portal
Core: every chapter join is one physical event (0.5–0.8 s) with its own sound, OUT of chapter N and IN of N+1 share a
seed. Axis: the form comes from the film's metaphor and is never the previous film's (QR → Tembus Selaput → …).

### S6 Physics and sound
Brand physics written before eases (ui T .45 ζ .85 · hero .55/.7 · heavy .8/.9 · playful .4/.5); snap → cushion
(85 % of travel in ~4 frames, settle ~20); every visual cause has a non-tonal sound; calm bed in chapters, driving
score in the intro only. Film-transition hits: see S8 (mandatory).

### S7 Jejak Riset (documentary research honesty, visible)
Source tab on every article and photo; labels ANALISIS REDAKSI / ILUSTRASI / PERKIRAAN where they apply; no invented
numbers; doubts shown with the lens-sweep audit. This is what "documentary research" looks like on screen: the
viewer can see where each claim comes from.

### S8 Hit Transisi Film (MANDATORY: Ikhsan 2026-11-15, "efek transisi film nya itu wajib")
Core (never dropped, in every film):
- The film-transition hits from `aset/_transisi` play through the whole video, not just the intro, each with its own
  sound. They come in through the template library (`tx/`, `tools/tx_lib.py`).
- Think Make Push packs come first.
- Use 4–5 per chapter, on whip joins and chapter joins. Never use one per station, and never repeat the previous hit
  (no spam).
- Land the hit's `peak` frame on the cut or the whip midpoint, picture and sound together.
- Hits keep only the accent hue band (S3, the `filter` per pack).
- Burn, sepia or projector stays a short hit, never the film's look ([[no-vintage-film-look]]).

Axis (the fresh part):
- **The cuts.** Both hits used the same 9 cuts from 4 packs (stk1–4 light streaks, cmc1–2 comic, ldr1 film leader,
  shD/shE shutter). Indo vs AS references hits ≈ 26 times in code, AI Bubble ≈ 45 times.
  - Every new film cuts **≥ 2 new hits**: first from the unused Think Make Push packs (grunge film transitions,
    handwriting film overlay), then the rest (cinematic overlay, dark dust, light leak, film burn as a short hit).
  - Check the folder first for packs Ikhsan has added.
  - The old 9 stay as the base, and the new ones are the film's headline hits.
- **Placement.** A hit may also be the punctuation inside a long travel (§6 point 1), not only on a join.
- **Direction.** Match the streak or wipe direction to the camera whip (mirror the clip if needed).

## 3. Upgrades of his two techniques with the new knowledge (candidates: each needs a 3-second test)

### 3a Satu Baris v2, "Baris Lensa" (the row as a real lens)
What exists: `intro/src/film.js` `rowC` + `drawAsset` (blur 84·(d − .03), soft key light, contact shadow, gate weave);
`kit.js` `onPlane` blur = |z − foc| / z · 24, already the right thin-lens shape (∝ |1/z − 1/z_f|).
Add, cheapest first:
1. **Rack focus on the word.** The focus plane moves on the VO word that names the item (0.25–0.35 s, inOutCubic),
   not on a timer; the eye trace is the focus pull.
2. **Focus breathing.** While focus travels, scale the whole frame 1–2 % (real lenses breathe); it reads as glass.
3. **Energy-conserving bokeh.** Specular points and FG motes grow with defocus and dim by 1/area (warm white, never
   lime): `defocus-dust-energy`. A mote crossing the lens on the lock gives the depth a foreground.
4. **Air between planes.** Thin fog sheets between depth planes, colour = the room's horizon colour
   (`horizon-matched-fog`), so the row shares one air.
5. **Speed ramp by integration.** Rush between items, slow-mo on the lock: rate r(t) 1 → .15 → 1, position = ∫ r dt
   (`speed-ramp-integrated`), never a jump in local time.
6. **True thin-lens DOF** (`thin-lens-accum-dof`) needs the engine change `scene.draw(lctx, t, i, K)` (proposed, not
   applied): each motion-blur subframe also samples the lens, so occlusion and bokeh shape are right.
Path axis recipes: racing-line rail (`racing-line-rail`, speed follows curvature, retimed to VO); helix = items on a
cylinder around the camera axis, camera rolls 4–6° into the turn.

### 3b Bukti Stabilo v2, "Bukti Hidup" (the evidence is alive)
What exists: `kit.js` `evidFull` (page on rotateY strips, 62° → 21° outQuint, drift −8° + 6 % push, ragged lime quad
with `multiply`, whip off).
Add:
1. **Stroke as ink, not a quad.** A pressure envelope along the stroke: a wet start blob (first 4 % wider and denser),
   steady body, dry tail (alpha breaks up in the last 15 % with seeded hash gaps); ink pools at both ends (second
   `multiply` pass, ~1.3× density); edge noise from a seeded hash; baseline wobble ≤ 1.5 px. Text stays black under it
   (`multiply` on light pages, `screen` at .5 on dark ones).
2. **A hand draws it** (`ghost-hand-phase-table`): a marker tip (or cursor) approaches 0.25 s → press (2 frames, page
   dips 0.5°) → drag for the word's duration → lift. Sound: dry felt scratch (non-tonal noise), never a squeak.
3. **The camera reads.** During the stroke the camera glides along the line (rail, brake on the last word); focus
   falls off with distance from the highlighted line (blur by page row), so the eye goes where the narrator is.
4. **Page as material.** A soft sheen band travels across the page as it turns (one softbox card, angle-driven,
   `softbox-card-studio` idea in 2D); contact shadow under the page. Digital page, never paper relief.
5. **Kutipan Lepas.** After the stroke the highlighted phrase lifts off the page as type (arrive light grey → settle
   ink, from the Drake bio ref), the page sinks back into blur, and the phrase becomes the next station's title. The
   evidence literally becomes the claim; that is the continuity a research doc needs.
6. **Exits that grow from the page:** `shadow-first-luma-wipe` into the next photo, or `tone-layer-lift` revealing the
   data card under the page.
7. **Many sources:** a fanned stack, or `sphere-gallery-flatten` (camera inside a sphere of sources, one flattens into
   focus) as a source-heavy chapter's opener.

### 3c Typography physics (video-9 rigs + Drake bio ref + motion-taste)
- **Proximity rig** (`loupe-magnet-proximity`): a visible prop is the null (stabilo tip, loupe, cursor); letters within
  R ≈ 230 px scale `ease(d, 0, R, 1.6, 1)` and are pushed out of line; letters already passed keep half the kick, so it
  stays a pure function of t. Punctuation is never kicked.
- **Escapement tick** (`beat-locked-watch-tick`): counters and clocks step `(floor(t) + pow(frac, 0.2)) · step` with a
  damped recoil, never a linear roll.
- **Arrive soft, settle ink** (Drake bio ref): a new word arrives light grey and turns ink as the VO lands it.
- **Object inside the type** (Drake bio ref): the fact object sits between two characters; the characters sweep in from
  behind it (~10 frames) and it casts a drop shadow on them.
- **Strike pendulum** (`strike-list-pendulum`): named items swing θ = A·e^(−age/τ)·sin(ω·age) when the VO names them.
- **Ghost trail + rush-hang-rush** for one chapter word per film; **curved smear** only at peak speed, ≤ 1 per chapter.
- Timing: per letter ±3 frames; container/content overlap 2–3 frames; exits 60–75 % of the entry time.

## 4. Premium checklist (per station, checked at the Studio pass)
1. **Depth:** ≥ 3 planes (FG mote/bokeh or edge element · subject · blurred BG); focus moves on a word.
2. **Light:** one key + one rim that moves with the motion; glow only on real light sources; highlights desaturate
   before the tonemap; TPDF dither on dark gradients (`agx-desat-tpdf-finish`).
3. **Material:** at least one real material per chapter (glass, metal, ink, liquid, film) lit by softboxes, not a
   flat fill.
4. **Cause → effect → sound:** every landing makes something react (ripple, crack, swing, ink spread) and makes a
   non-tonal sound.
5. **The image decides the choreography:** code transitions between stations grow out of the picture (its shadows,
   contours, tones), not a pasted curtain. This is on top of the S8 film hits, never instead of them.
6. **Never still:** no static frame > 4 s; evidence readable ≥ 2.5 s; the first layer lands on frame 1 of a station.
7. **Real assets** from Ahri/Ahpo before code-drawn props (Penutup bakso lesson).
8. **Motif lock:** ≤ 3 motifs per film; menu techniques ≤ 2 per film.

## 6. Measured DNA of the two hits (targets for the next films)
Measured 2026-11-15 with `eye.py dna --fps 5` on the delivered masters (`aset/teknologi-indo-vs-as/build/eye_dna_v1/`,
`aset/ai-bubble-dompet/build/eye_dna_v1/`). The kiblat column comes from short-form references, so it is a direction
only. A long-form documentary should not chase 1.5-second shots.

| Metric | Indo vs AS | AI Bubble | Kiblat | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shot median | 3.4 s | 3.9 s | 1.5 s | fine for long-form |
| Uncut spans > 15 s (share of runtime) | 18 (55 %) | 19 (55 %) | — | **main lever**: longest 45 s / 43 s |
| Colour fields / ΔE between shots | 6 / 21.6 | 7 / **7.1** | 3 / — | AI Bubble reads monotone |
| L median / contrast | 3.4 / 44.0 | 3.1 / **27.9** | — / 49 | dark room = brand; contrast fell |
| Mud frames | 50 % | 60 % | 22 % | the dark room hit anyway, so no target |
| Neon frames / halo | 7 % / 0.26 % | 6 % / 0.22 % | 1 % | halo is tiny; likely the lime slabs (not checked per frame) |
| Burst / hold | 11 / 61 % | 13 / 61 % | 37 / 26 % | **lever**: few snaps between holds |
| Camera alive on holds | 90 % | 95 % | — | keep |
| Cuts on an audio onset | 45 % | 60 % | — | raise |

What the numbers say (signals, not verdicts: both films were hits):
1. **The camera never stops, but the picture rarely punctuates.** More than half of each film sits in uncut travels
   longer than 15 s. The camera is alive (90–95 %), yet the eye gets no new "sentence" for 15–45 s. This is the most
   likely source of "penonton mulai bosan". The fix is punctuation inside the travel, not shorter evidence holds: a
   rack focus on the VO word (3a), a rush-hang-rush (L3), a hit, a cut-in to a detail, or a tone-layer lift.
2. **Snaps are rare.** Burst is 11–13 %, against the house rule of at least 20 % ([[motion-tempo-fast]]). Add short
   whips and snap-to-hold moves between stations, and keep the holds readable.
3. **AI Bubble lost colour movement.** ΔE 7.1 against 21.6, and contrast 27.9 against 44: one dark-green room for 14
   minutes. Indo vs AS moved more because of its light-world beats (question planes, white paper, lime slabs). Each
   chapter needs a colour-script turn (`story-progress-colour-script`) inside the one-accent rule.
4. **Leave the dark room and the lime slabs alone.** Mud and neon read far from the kiblat, but this world is the
   channel's signature and both hits live in it. Win contrast inside the dark instead: an ivory key on the object,
   contact shadows, a lit page.
