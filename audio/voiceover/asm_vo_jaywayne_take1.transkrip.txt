Psychologists call that pull the compromise effect. Its famous cousin is the decoy. The
Economist once offered print only for the same price as print plus web. Nobody took print
only. But the bundle jumped from 32% to 84%. Wait, here's the twist. When researchers retested
decoys with real products, the effect mostly vanished. 11 solid results out of 91 tries.
