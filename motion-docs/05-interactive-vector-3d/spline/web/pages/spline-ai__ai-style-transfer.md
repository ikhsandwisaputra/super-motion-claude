# AI Style Transfer

<Callout type="warn">
  **AI Style Transfer is now deprecated.**
</Callout>

With AI Style Transfer you can generate images using your 3D scenes as a base.

It’s a great way to quickly visualize your ideas or expand your 3D compositions with extra details.

***

## How to use AI Style Transfer [#how-to-use-ai-style-transfer]

### Accessing AI Style Transfer [#accessing-ai-style-transfer]

The AI Style Transfer feature can be accessed from the toolbar, by pressing the AI button.

### Using AI Style Transfer [#using-ai-style-transfer]

Once you open AI Style Transfer, you’ll find a new inspector sidebar and you’ll also see the result of your first image generation. From there you can start tweaking the settings or changing your scene to achieve other results. Generation happens automatically in most cases, but you can always press the Generate button to generate a new image.

***

## Styles Panel [#styles-panel]

### Seed [#seed]

Each image generation uses a randomly generated seed. You can lock it to maintain the same result and tweak it slightly with prompts or modifiers.

### Prompt [#prompt]

Prompts are used to influence your image generations. In this input, you can type descriptive sentences or words of what you want your final image to look like.

### Style Presets [#style-presets]

These are presets you can use as a base for your image generation.

### Modifiers [#modifiers]

Modifiers are extra keywords you can toggle On to influence your final result.

### Advanced Settings [#advanced-settings]

Here you can find additional settings to get even more precision on your image generations.

* **Exclude** → Sentences or words of things that you don’t want to see in your generation.
* **Precision** → How many steps does the image generation take. The longer the more precise it will be but will also increase processing time.
* **Style** → How much do the selected style and prompts influence the generations.
* **Variance** → How much the generation takes into account the 3D scene.
* **Mode**
  * **Outline** - Based on the outline of your 3D scene.
  * **Edges** - A mix of outline and shadows.
  * **Shapes** - A normal map of your 3D scene

***

## Limitations [#limitations]

The development and research of artificial intelligence (AI) is an ongoing process with several factors that can limit its capabilities. You will likely find bugs and some issues, but we are improving the system every day. Your feedback can help us improve it.
