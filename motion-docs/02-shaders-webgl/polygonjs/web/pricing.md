## FAQ

Which one should I pick?
+

Take Polygon Web if you want to start today in a browser, and if the AI agent and the
generative nodes are part of why you are here, because those two run on our servers and
exist nowhere else. Take the perpetual license if you want the app and the SDK on your own
machine, with no renewal and no metering. Take Enterprise if the answer depends on a
contract, a pipeline or a room of people to train.

What does a credit buy?
+

One currency for everything metered.
An agent turn costs 1, a generated model 20.
Exporting a web app and exporting a 3D model cost no credits at all: they are included, with no monthly limit.
A plan includes 200 credits a month, which is 10 generated models, or 200 agent turns, or any mix of the two.
Credits do not roll over.

How can I use the SDK?
+

You need access to the Jai language and compiler. The language is not publicly released yet,
but we're happy to point you to who to contact to enter the beta.
[Get in touch](mailto:gui@polygonjs.com?subject=Polygon%20SDK)
if you're not sure how.

I have purchased v1. Can I renew at the same price?
+

Yes. If you purchased the first version of polygon, you'll see a link in your
[dashboard](https://polygonjs.com/dashboard) to renew at the same price.

Can I cancel Polygon Web?
+

Any time, from your [dashboard](https://polygonjs.com/dashboard/account).
Cancelling stops the next renewal. Nothing is charged again and nothing is refunded for the
period you are in.

What happens when the period ends?
+

You keep the paid features until the last day you paid for. After that the account goes back
to the free web editor: your scenes stay where they are and stay editable, and the files you
already exported are yours and are not touched. Included credits do not roll over.

Does Polygon Web include the app I install?
+

No. The two are sold separately. Polygon Web is the editor in the browser. The desktop app
and the SDK come with the perpetual license, and a subscription never turns into one.

Do the prices include tax?
+

Prices are shown before tax. Where we have to collect VAT or a sales tax, Stripe works it out
from the address you give at checkout and adds it there, so the total you approve is the
total you pay.
