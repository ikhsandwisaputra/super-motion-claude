## Why smooth scroll?

Lenis runs the web

What began as an internal tool for syncing WebGL and the DOM is now the default smooth scroll across the industry — even powering libraries like [Locomotive Scroll](https://github.com/locomotivemtl/locomotive-scroll). The smoothness was a happy accident. So we open-sourced the whole thing.

## As it should be

Lenis is a free, [open-source library](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis) that turns native scroll into a silky, controllable experience in a single line of code. Go no-code with the [Framer plugin](https://framer.link/cHWxQZf), and keep everything accessible and under 5kb. Made by [darkroom.engineering](https://darkroom.engineering).

Using Lenis in your project? [Sponsor it](https://lenis.dev/sponsor) or [work with darkroom](https://darkroom.engineering/contact).
